RUNORA – PHOTO FOLDER
=====================

Put five photos in this folder, named exactly as below (all lowercase, .jpg):

  hero.jpg      Full-width background behind the logo.
                A runner in a city at first light. Landscape, wide.
                Minimum 2000 px wide. The left side is covered by a dark
                overlay, so keep the subject on the right of the frame.

  fitting.jpg   Gait analysis / shoe fitting. Landscape, very wide (21:9 crop).
                Side-on view, feet and lower legs, treadmill visible.
                Minimum 2000 px wide.

  store.jpg     Shoe wall or store interior. Landscape, very wide (21:9 crop).
                No readable brand logos anywhere in the frame.
                Minimum 2000 px wide.

  trail.jpg     Trail running, mountains or rocky terrain. PORTRAIT (4:5).
                Minimum 1200 px wide.

  club.jpg      A group of runners together. PORTRAIT (4:5).
                Shoot this one yourself – Vortex on the boulevard.
                Minimum 1200 px wide.

RULES
-----
1. No readable brand logos (Nike, Asics, Hoka and so on) in any photo.
2. Avoid scenery that is obviously not the Caucasus or a city like Baku.
3. Free, licensed sources: unsplash.com, pexels.com, pixabay.com
4. Save each as .jpg, quality 80, under about 500 KB so the page loads fast.

Until a file is added, that slot shows a plain panel with the filename on it.
Add the file, push to GitHub, and it appears automatically. Nothing else to change.
